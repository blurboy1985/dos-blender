MAKE YOURSELF AT HOME

Your visitor guide.

Move with W A S D or the arrow keys. The camera follows behind your full-body avatar. Drag to turn the camera; scroll to change its distance. On a phone, hold the arrow buttons to walk. Use + and − to zoom, the curved arrows to turn, and Reset to restore the view. You can also drag to look or pinch to zoom. Near walls, the camera automatically moves closer to keep your view inside the office.

Select any gallery on the left to go straight there. Approach a seated officer and press T to open their contact options. Press E or select “Explore this gallery” to read an exhibit and try its activity. G visits the next gallery; H returns to reception.

“View floor plan” gives you an overview: drag to orbit, then pinch or use the zoom buttons to change the view. This is a WebGL adaptation of the Blender model. The downloadable Unreal project still requires an Unreal build and play-test.

A conceptual learning experience based on public DOS resources. Framed wall displays use dated SingStat data and guidance; open any gallery to enlarge its display and view its source. The original unframed chart bars are illustrative. This site does not accept survey responses.
